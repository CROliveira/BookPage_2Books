Frequently Asked

Common questions

How do I get an autographed copy of Constitutional Intelligence or Étoile de Tournesol?

Order directly through fedethics.ca/books using any payment button. Every print order placed through this site is personally autographed by Celio before shipping — at no extra charge.

How does the free consultation offer work for bulk book purchases?

Purchase 5 or more copies of either book through this site, then complete the claim form on the books page. We will confirm your session within 48 hours. The offer is redeemable as a virtual call or a live speaking engagement.

Can Celio speak at our government department, university, or corporate event?

Yes. Celio accepts speaking engagements internationally. Use the contact form on this page or email celio.oliveira@fedethics.ca with your event date, location, audience size, and topic focus.

Can we order books on invoice for our institution or government department?

Yes. Institutional orders can be arranged with a formal invoice. Email communications@fedethics.ca with your required quantity, delivery address, and billing information.

Is Celio available for media interviews or podcast appearances?

Yes. Celio is available for interviews on AI governance, ethical leadership, data strategy, and both books. Use the contact form and select "Media / Press Interview" or "Podcast Appearance" as your enquiry type.

Comment puis-je commander Étoile de Tournesol pour ma classe ou mon club de lecture?

Commandez directement via la page du livre. Pour les commandes de 5 exemplaires ou plus, vous débloquez une rencontre de 15 minutes avec l'auteur et la possibilité d'obtenir une dédicace personnalisée pour chaque lecteur.

What is FedEthics Inc. and who is it independent from?

FedEthics Inc. is Celio Oliveira's independent advisory and publishing company. All work published and all advisory services delivered through FedEthics are in a strictly personal capacity, independent of any government institution or employer.

How quickly will I receive a response?

We respond to all enquiries within 48 business hours. For time-sensitive requests, email celio.oliveira@fedethics.ca directly with "URGENT" in the subject line.
