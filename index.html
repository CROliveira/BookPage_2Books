Digital Democracy

Executive Guide on Parliamentary Principles for AI Excellence

📅 May 2026 ● Forthcoming ● FedEthics Advisory™

A landmark guide for public sector executives, legislators, and institutional leaders navigating the governance of artificial intelligence in parliamentary democracies. Drawing on 15+ years of federal AI and data leadership across Canada's largest institutions, this book provides a practical, principled architecture for responsible AI adoption — grounded in democratic values, public accountability, and human-centred design.

Recognized as a defining contribution to the field by some of Canada's most senior federal leaders, this book arrives at a pivotal moment for institutions worldwide.

Étoile de Tournesol

Sunflower Star — A Story of Light, Roots, and Becoming

📅 May 2026 ● Forthcoming ● FedEthics Creative™
"Like a sunflower, she does not chase the light in desperation — she turns toward it with the quiet certainty that it will come."

Étoile de Tournesol is a luminous work of literary fiction and personal reflection — a story of migration, belonging, love, and the quiet dignity of becoming. Drawing from the richness of multiple cultures and languages, it speaks to anyone who has ever searched for home in an unfamiliar sky.

Published under FedEthics Creative™ — the literary imprint of FedEthics Inc. — this work affirms that the same mind that architects governance frameworks can also hold tenderness, grief, and wonder. The sunflower always finds its light.
